## About

Astro Raider is a 2D pixel art roguelike endless shooter built in Godot 4. You drill through destructible terrain, collect crystals, and spend them at the Generator to unlock and upgrade perks — all while defending against escalating waves of enemies and bosses.

Every run pushes you to make meaningful build decisions. Pick your perks, adapt to what the game throws at you, and see how far you can hold out.

## Features

- **Destructible terrain** — drill through the environment to mine resources and find your path.
- **Perk system** — choose and upgrade perks across passive, active, and ultimate categories with rarity-weighted selection.
- **Combat** — ranged weapons, drill attacks, shields, knockback, and status effects.
- **Enemy variety** — distinct AI behaviors, environmental hazards, and multi-phase boss encounters.
- **Procedural worlds** — each planet has its own layout, enemy set, and difficulty config.
