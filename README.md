## About

Astro Raider is a 2D pixel art roguelike shooter built in Godot 4. Each run lasts around 15–20 minutes — you spawn on a hostile planet, drill through destructible terrain, collect crystals, and spend them at the Generator to unlock and upgrade perks. Survive the enemy waves, defeat the final boss, and move on.

## Features

- **Destructible terrain** — drill through the environment to mine crystals and carve your own path.
- **Perk system** — choose and upgrade perks across passive, active, and ultimate categories with rarity-weighted selection.
- **Suit system** — pick a suit before each run. Each suit defines your base stats, scaling bonuses per meta-level, and an evolving weapon mechanic.
- **Combat** — ranged weapons, drill attacks, shields, knockback, and status effects.
- **Enemy variety** — distinct AI behaviors, environmental hazards, and multi-phase boss encounters.
- **Procedural worlds** — each planet has its own layout, enemy set, and difficulty configuration.
- **Tower defense elements** — place and upgrade turrets, platforms, and repair drones at the Generator.
